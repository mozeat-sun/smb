# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
collaborative, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

## Expected Behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Assume good intent and communicate clearly.
- Focus criticism on ideas, not people.
- Welcome newcomers and diverse perspectives.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or hate speech.
- Personal attacks, insults, or trolling.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Any conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, and other
interactions that violate this Code of Conduct.

## Reporting

To report misconduct, open a private security-style report as described in
SECURITY.md, or contact maintainers through repository owner channels.
